## Executive finding

EXP-A establishes a behavioral source-trust rule more strongly than it establishes a parser-library winner:

> **Critical number presence is insufficient. Table-derived facts must preserve or reconstruct row, column, unit and period/metric roles; otherwise the document is not source-ready.**

On the three manually checked public primary-source fixtures, a structured-reference extraction passes all critical anchors and table-role checks. A deliberately flat-text fallback retains 100% of critical numeric values but becomes `REVIEW_REQUIRED` on both table-bearing PDFs because table-role provenance is absent. An adversarial role swap on the earnings fixture keeps every critical numeric token present yet correctly becomes `FAIL` with `silent_corruption=true`.

Therefore **no parser vendor/library is locked by this attempt**. The provisional strategy is contract-first: accept any parser only if it emits stable provenance and passes deterministic source-trust gates; use flat text only as a fallback that cannot silently promote table-derived facts to `SOURCE_READY`.

## Recommendation / kill criteria

### Provisional strategy

1. Keep `ParserAdapter` vendor-neutral.
2. Require stable document/span provenance and raw source hash when raw bytes are available.
3. For table-derived anchors require `table`, `row`, `column`, `unit`, and period/metric role before `SOURCE_READY`.
4. Permit flat/native text as a fallback only when no critical fact depends on lost structure; otherwise route to `REVIEW_REQUIRED` or a layout/table parser.
5. Treat parser warnings and ambiguous extraction as first-class source-trust state, not log noise.

### Kill a parser/fallback if any is true

- a critical value is missing or altered;
- all values are present but one is bound to the wrong metric/entity/period;
- table-derived values lack reconstructible row/column/unit provenance;
- material qualifier/modality is dropped;
- extraction ambiguity is not surfaced;
- repeated extraction of identical bytes is nondeterministic without an explicit reason;
- source/hash provenance cannot be attached to downstream spans.

### Do not lock yet

This three-document bakeoff proves the hard-gate behavior and a negative baseline, but it does not justify a permanent library/vendor lock. Before final lock, replay raw bytes over a larger development corpus with multiple table layouts, record raw SHA-256, compare at least two viable PDF extraction stacks, and measure role-preservation/error rates.
