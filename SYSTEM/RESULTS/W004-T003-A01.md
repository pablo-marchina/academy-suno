## Executive finding

The expanded bakeoff strengthens the earlier source-trust rule across three materially different primary-source families:

> **A parser cannot be source-ready merely because it retained every value. For table/structured facts, table, row, column, unit and period roles are part of the fact and must survive extraction.**

The experiment adds current fixtures for Copom, CVM ITR schema evolution and issuer earnings/results. Across all three fixtures, a provenance-preserving reference passes. A value-only extraction retains 100% of golden values but is held at `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. Deliberate wrong-role mutations retain 100% of golden values yet fail in all three families. A cross-table unit mutation on the earnings fixture (`R$ milhões` -> `US$ milhões`) also fails while keeping value coverage at 100%.

This separates **safe vs unsafe extraction behaviour** but does **not** separate production parser libraries. Parser implementation identity therefore remains `UNLOCKED`, exactly as required by the dispatch.

## Expanded primary-source corpus

### 1. Copom — 279th meeting

Primary source: Banco Central do Brasil, *Ata da 279ª Reunião do Copom*  
URL: https://www.bcb.gov.br/publicacoes/atascopom/17062026

Golden table roles cover the official reference-scenario inflation table, including:

- IPCA 2026 = `5.2`, bound to the 2026 projection column;
- IPCA fourth-quarter 2027 = `3.7`, bound to the `4T2027` period role;
- administered-price IPCA fourth-quarter 2027 = `3.9`, preserving a distinct row role from headline IPCA;
- unit provenance = four-quarter accumulated percent.

Adversarial mutation: keep the `5.2` value but bind it to `4T2027` instead of 2026. Expected and observed result: `FAIL`, `silent_corruption=true`.

### 2. CVM — 2026 ITR resource + schema change

Primary dataset: CVM, *Cias Abertas: Documentos: Formulário de Informações Trimestrais (ITR)*  
URL: https://dados.cvm.gov.br/dataset/cia_aberta-doc-itr

Primary schema-change notice: CVM/SEP, 19 Aug 2026  
URL: https://www.gov.br/cvm/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2026/area-tecnica-da-cvm-orienta-sobre-alteracoes-nos-formularios-de-informacoes-trimestrais-e-de-demonstracoes-financeiras-padronizadas

The official dataset identifies ITR as a periodic electronic filing and exposes a 2026 ZIP resource. The CVM notice states that from 29 Aug 2026 the `Dados da Empresa` section gained two mandatory fields, `Valor da Receita Bruta Consolidada Acumulada` and `Data`, and missing them blocks submissions/re-submissions.

This fixture intentionally tests **schema/field-role preservation**, not issuer-specific accounting values. Both mandatory-field anchors have the same literal state (`required`), which makes it a useful silent-corruption case: swapping the field identity still preserves all value tokens but must fail provenance.

### 3. Petrobras — 1T26 results

Primary issuer PDF: Petrobras, *Relatório de Desempenho — 1T26*  
URL: https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/25fdf098-34f5-4608-b7fa-17d60b2de47d/c59102ba-ab78-db77-c32c-ba5923cfa6ff?origin=2

Official discovery page: https://agencia.petrobras.com.br/w/negocio/petrobras-registra-lucro-l%C3%ADquido-de-r-32-7-bilh%C3%B5es-no-primeiro-trimestre-de-2026

Table 1 and Table 3 were visually checked from the official PDF before fixture construction. Golden bindings include:

- sales revenue 1T26 = `123686`, Table 1, `R$ milhões`;
- Petrobras-shareholder net income 1T26 = `32663`, Table 1, `R$ milhões`;
- adjusted EBITDA 1T26 = `59643`, Table 1, `R$ milhões`;
- total investments 1T26 = `5107`, Table 3, `US$ milhões`;
- total investments 4T25 = `6313`, Table 3, `US$ milhões` with prior-quarter role.

Two adversarial failures are exercised: net-income row relabelled as adjusted EBITDA, and sales revenue incorrectly rebound from Table 1/BRL million to Table 3/USD million. Both retain the original numeric value and correctly emit `FAIL` + `silent_corruption=true`.

## Source-trust contract exercised

The harness treats these as non-compensating hard gates:

1. critical value must be present and exact;
2. table-derived facts require exact table/row/column roles;
3. unit provenance must be exact when specified;
4. period role must be exact;
5. missing provenance cannot be promoted to `PASS`;
6. wrong non-null provenance is a `FAIL` even at 100% value coverage.

This preserves, rather than weakens, the W002 source-trust posture.
