## Summary

Built B13 as a read-only evidence cockpit over existing RunStore / telemetry / calibration / proof artifacts. The implementation does not introduce a second evidence truth store; it derives a cockpit snapshot from versioned evidence artifacts and renders the selected evidence with explicit unknown/failure states and provenance.

## Acceptance coverage

- Explicit evidence states implemented: `PROVEN`, `DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY`, `NOT_COMPUTABLE`, `NOT_RUN`, `NOT_COMPARABLE`, `PRODUCTION_UNKNOWN`, `FAIL`, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
- Source/run/job/attempt provenance is preserved in the cockpit data model and rendered trace panels.
- 3×3 audience × format comparison is rendered with per-cell state and evidence details.
- Source traceability is rendered separately from generated output evidence.
- Repair lineage renders before/after hashes/evaluations plus resolved/introduced failure codes and hard-gate/sibling invariants.
- Telemetry view exposes retries, quality repairs, usage, latency and cost evidence posture.
- Synthetic pricing remains diagnostic and is not promoted to provider-cost evidence.
- Missing/N/A/unknown evidence remains explicit; no aggregate readiness score is computed.
- Hard `FAIL` and `REVIEW_REQUIRED` states remain visible and are never compensated by unrelated positive evidence.
