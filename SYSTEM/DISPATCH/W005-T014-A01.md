## Objective

Systematically research and benchmark the production document-ingestion/parsing/extraction layer for public financial PDFs/text so source grounding, table semantics and provenance are selected by evidence rather than parser familiarity. The existing W004 parser/source-trust behavior is the mandatory baseline and fail-closed source trust remains invariant.

## Scope in

- materially different parsing/document-intelligence approaches, including credible local/open-source and managed/document-AI classes;
- text, page, section and heading preservation;
- tables: row/column roles, headers, units, footnotes, merged cells and semantic table-role provenance;
- preservation/extraction of numbers, dates, entities and material concepts with page/region anchors;
- scanned/OCR documents and image-only pages where relevant;
- complex, malformed, encrypted/unsupported and large PDFs;
- safe upload/parser boundary and parser sandbox/resource-limit considerations;
- latency, throughput, memory/CPU, cost and operational burden;
- deterministic confidence/fail-closed behavior for ambiguous extraction;
- compatibility with factual/source evaluator and live source explorer;
- migration path from W004 ingest/parser adapters.

- do not choose object storage/auth/database on behalf of T004;
- do not select provider/model generation strategy on behalf of T006;
- do not bypass source-trust or factual hard gates to make a parser look better;
- do not use OCR automatically when native extraction is sufficient merely to increase stack complexity;
- do not lock a parser/document-AI vendor without representative evidence.
